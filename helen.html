Helen's Bridge









Helen's Bridge is a bridge that was built in 1909 on Asheville's Beaucatcher Mountain. The speculation is that a beautiful woman who was the servant of the house owner became pregnant. She couldn't accept the pregnancy or hidden love affair so she committed suicide on that very bridge.

Another possibility was that the woman Helen lost her daughter in a fire at a nearby place and now haunts the location.

There have been many sightings of Helen, hearing her scream and issues with mechanical devices such as car radios, emf detectors and flashlights.



Our Findings:

While hunting in this location, I happened to be 30 weeks pregnant with our son. Andrew and I had gotten some weak directions of go up Beaucatcher's Mountain and you'll find it. I was feeling pulled by every turn and fork in the road and became overwhelmed by a strong feeling. I told Andrew, "We are here." We both gazed up at the large, mossy facade of a bridge. I noticed what looked to be someone walking around us, but it was like a shadow.

We pulled out our EMF and began taking readings. The readings were in the red and screaming. 400-500mW/m^2 and holding steady. I felt we had to leave, something wasn't right. We left the area, car zig zagging down the mountain. I had looked into the backseat and saw another shadow figure, I told Andrew someone was here with us. The EMF detector was still screaming the entire time, I was incredibly scared. The figure didn't move or make a sound. Finally, as we turned the last turn off the mountain, the figure disappeared! I said "Oh my God, it's gone." Then the EMF went silent. Normal readings of 0-5 resumed in an instant.
